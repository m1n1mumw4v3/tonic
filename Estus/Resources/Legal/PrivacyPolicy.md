# Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 14, 2026

Estus ("we", "us", "our") provides a personalized supplement-planning service through the Estus mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

## Information We Collect

**Account information.** When you create an account we collect your name (optional), email address, and authentication credentials. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the identifiers and email address those services share with us.

**Health profile.** During onboarding and ongoing use, you may provide health-related information including: age, sex, height and weight, health goals, current supplements, allergies, medications, dietary preferences, exercise frequency, caffeine and alcohol intake, stress level, baseline wellness scores, and reproductive-health status (such as pregnancy, breastfeeding, hormonal birth control, or hormone replacement therapy). You may also complete optional "deep profile" modules covering sleep, hormonal, gut, cognitive, musculoskeletal, and stress topics.

**Daily check-ins.** Each day you use the App you may log how you feel across five wellness dimensions (sleep, energy, clarity, mood, gut) along with notes.

**Apple Health data.** If you connect Apple Health, we read the categories you authorize (such as sleep, activity, and heart-rate metrics) to personalize your plan. We do not write to Apple Health.

**Technical information.** We collect basic device information (device model, iOS version, app version, anonymous app-install identifier) for diagnostics and crash reporting.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to:

- Generate and refine your personalized supplement plan
- Track wellness trends and produce insights based on your check-ins
- Provide customer support and respond to your requests
- Operate, secure, and improve the App
- Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your health information for advertising.

## Where Your Data Is Stored

Some data is stored on your device. Other data is stored in our backend infrastructure operated by our subprocessors. Account and profile data is associated with your account so that it is available across reinstalls and devices once you sign in.

## Third-Party Services

We rely on the following third parties to operate the App:

- **Supabase** — authentication and database hosting
- **Apple App Store** and **RevenueCat** — subscription management and receipt validation (when subscriptions are active)
- **PostHog** — anonymized product analytics, when enabled
- **Apple HealthKit** — only when you grant permission and only for categories you authorize

Each third party processes data subject to its own privacy practices. We disclose only what is necessary for the service those providers perform for us.

## Data Retention

We retain your account and health data for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account and associated data at any time through Settings → Delete Account & Data. After deletion we remove your data from our active systems and from backups during the normal backup-rotation cycle (typically within 30 days).

## Your Choices and Rights

You can:

- Review and edit your profile, goals, and health information in Settings
- Disconnect Apple Health at any time from the App or from iOS Settings
- Request a copy of your data by emailing us
- Delete your account and data from Settings

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR (EU/UK), CCPA (California), or similar regulations — including the right to access, correct, port, restrict, or delete your information, and the right to lodge a complaint with a data-protection authority.

## Children

The App is intended for adults aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

## Security

We use industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, however, and we cannot guarantee the absolute security of your data.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through the App or by email. The "Last Updated" date above will always reflect the most recent revision.
